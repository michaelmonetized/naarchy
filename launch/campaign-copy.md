# Naarchy launch

Your files. Your clipboard. Your music. Your day.

One little island at the top of your Linux desktop. Click it. Carry on.

Naarchy is free, open source, and built with Rust + GTK4 for Omarchy and Hyprland. Clipboard and shelf content stay on your machine. No account. No telemetry.

# Inbox feature post

Put it down. Pick it up.

Drop a file, image, or bit of text into Naarchy's Inbox. Pin what matters. Drag it back when you need it. Your original files stay where they belong.

Free. MIT licensed. Source included.

# Clipboard feature post

Copied. Never lost.

Find the text or image you copied earlier. Search your history. Keep favorites. Copy it again.

The history stays on your machine. It is not encrypted; keep that in mind when copying private material.

Free software. No account. No telemetry.
